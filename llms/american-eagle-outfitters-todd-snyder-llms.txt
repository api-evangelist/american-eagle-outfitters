Todd Snyder is a premium American menswear brand and ecommerce store selling modern tailored clothing, casual apparel, footwear, accessories, and seasonal wardrobe essentials.

Core Value Proposition:
Todd Snyder offers elevated menswear that blends classic tailoring, American sportswear, refined casualwear, and modern design. The store helps customers build a polished, versatile wardrobe across suiting, denim, outerwear, shirting, shoes, knitwear, and accessories.

Unique Positioning:

* Designer menswear with a modern American point of view
* Broad wardrobe coverage from tailored suits to casual essentials
* Seasonal collections, runway collections, collaborations, and limited product drops
* Ecommerce experience supported by Shopify
* Product discovery through collections, editorial merchandising, search, filters, recommendations, and style-focused content
* Customer service support for sizing, shipping, returns, exchanges, stores, gift cards, and order assistance

The primary purpose of toddsnyder.com is to sell Todd Snyder menswear and related products online.

The store serves:

Customer-Facing Information

Shipping:
Todd Snyder offers shipping options displayed at checkout.

Domestic Shipping:

* Free standard shipping is available on orders over $200 within the Continental United States.
* Standard shipping for orders under $200 is $9.
* Expedited shipping is available for a flat fee where eligible.
* Rush shipping is available for a flat fee where eligible.
* Orders placed after 12 PM EST may be processed on the following business day.
* Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays are not considered business days.
* Expedited and Rush shipping may not be available to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Territories, APO/FPO, or P.O. Boxes.

International Shipping:

* International shipping costs vary by destination country and order weight.
* International options and costs are shown at checkout after shipping address entry.
* Import duties and taxes may be calculated at checkout and included in the final order total.
* International returns are not free.
* Duties and taxes paid at checkout may be non-refundable.
* Some store-fulfilled online merchandise may not be available for international shipping.

Shipping Source of Truth:
https://www.toddsnyder.com/pages/shipping-policy

Returns:

* Returns can be made in store or online for eligible products.
* Eligible exchange and return items must be received within 30 days of purchase.
* Items marked Final Sale, Final Markdown, or Clearance are non-returnable.
* Returned products must be new, unused, in original packaging, and have Todd Snyder and designer garment tags attached.
* Shipping costs are not refundable.
* Returns are subject to a $7 restocking or return label fee where applicable.
* In-store returns are free where eligible.
* Store credit may not be changed or transferred after the return has been completed.
* Refunds may take additional time to process after the Returns Department receives the item.

Returns Source of Truth:
https://www.toddsnyder.com/pages/returns

Returns Portal:
https://returns.toddsnyder.com/

Exchanges:

* Exchanges are available for eligible items.
* Online exchange options may be limited to a different size in the same style and color.
* Footwear exchanges may ship after the warehouse has fulfilled the return for security purposes.

URL & Content Interpretation Rules

Canonical Product URL:
https://www.toddsnyder.com/products/[product-handle]

Canonical Collection URL:
https://www.toddsnyder.com/collections/[collection-handle]

Product Handles:
Product handles are unique URL-safe identifiers for products.

Collection Handles:
Collection handles are unique URL-safe identifiers for product groupings.

Variant IDs:
Variant IDs identify specific combinations of size, color, fit, or other product options.

SKU:
SKUs may identify sellable product variants. SKU formatting may vary by product, season, collaboration, and system.

Product Tags:
Tags may support merchandising, filtering, collections, feeds, search, automation, and internal operations. Not all tags are customer-facing.

Metafields:
Metafields may contain additional structured product, collection, customer, order, or content data. Not all metafields are publicly available.

Metaobjects:
Metaobjects may power structured content such as size guides, product attributes, editorial content, landing pages, recommendations, or reusable content blocks.

Search and Filter URLs:
Filtered, sorted, or parameterized collection URLs may be dynamic and may be disallowed or limited by robots.txt. Prefer canonical collection and product URLs for stable references.
